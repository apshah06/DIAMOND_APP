Add supplier

Suppliers you add here show up in the dropdown when taking a stone on jangaad. Each gets a permanent ID for future linking/analysis.

Add customer

Customers you add here show up in the dropdown when giving a stone on jangaad to a customer. Each gets a permanent ID for future linking/analysis.

Give stone to customer

Records which customer currently has this stone on jangaad. It stays in Stock either way — this is just who has it right now.

Take stone on jangaad

Records a stone taken on memo from a supplier — it appears in Stock until converted to a purchase or returned.

Convert to purchase

Pick a stone currently held on jangaad — converting it records the purchase and removes it from jangaad stock.
Payment status updates automatically from Cash / Bank entries.

Return stones to supplier

These stones leave Stock and are logged in Returns for your records.

Convert stones to purchase

Applies the same commercial terms to every selected stone (Rap still looks up per-stone when Auto is on). Each stone keeps its own size, so totals are calculated individually.
Payment status updates automatically from Cash / Bank entries.

Sell to customer

Same rate structure as a purchase. Records the sale against this purchase and calculates profit/loss automatically.
Payment status updates automatically from Cash / Bank entries.

Add entry

Debit is money going out, Credit is money coming in. Balance is calculated automatically.
